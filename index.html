⚙️ Bot Engineering · Business Intelligence · 2025
FUT BLACK MARKET — DISCORD MEMBER INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM
Built a custom Discord bot using Python + discord.py that automatically scrapes YAGPDB role-assignment embed messages from a private #log channel, parses subscription events with regex, and compiles a full 3,500+ member database into a structured multi-sheet Excel report — completely automated end-to-end. The bot handles incremental state via a JSON checkpoint file, resolves member identity across nickname changes, tracks every subscription lifecycle (start/end/renewal), and exports 4 production-grade sheets: All_Members, Premium_Subscriptions, Summary_Stats, and Monthly_Income. Analysis of the resulting data revealed churn patterns and peak acquisition windows, directly informing a pricing and retention strategy that drove a 30% uplift in new subscriptions.
